# Páez Ville — Reference Bible (real Córdoba / Villa Páez / Alberdi history)

> The game's premise is that history surfaces through conversation. These are the **verified real
> references** to weave into dialogue, signs, NPC barks, and map details. **Distinction (per brief §7.5):
> mark what's documented history vs. dramatisation.** The v0.1 happy-path uses these as local-color
> sketches — when the story is rewritten post-tag, the cited history here is the spine.
>
> Sources listed per entry. Researched 2026-08-09.

## 1. Villa Páez (the barrio the game nods to)

- **Location:** centro-oeste of Córdoba Capital, **bordering the Río Suquía** to the north. Borders:
  *Alberdi Bajo, Galán, Marichal, Escobar* and others. Northwest of the city center, very close to downtown.
  — [AlFilo Nº16 (FFyH UNC)](https://ffyh.unc.edu.ar/alfilo/anteriores/alfilo-16/relacion_con_la_comunidad.htm)
  · ["El Comunero" Relata Pt.2 (Comechingones del Pueblo de la Toma)](https://www.facebook.com/comechingonesdelpueblodelatoma/videos/)
- **Age:** exists since the **1920s**, later consolidated as a formal barrio. A centennial neighborhood.
  — [AlFilo Nº16](https://ffyh.unc.edu.ar/alfilo/anteriores/alfilo-16/relacion_con_la_comunidad.htm)
  · [Breve Historia de Villa Páez (Pueblo Alberdi, UNC)](https://puebloalberdi.sociales.unc.edu.ar/breve-historia-de-villa-paez/)
- **Name:** from the **Páez family**, an affluent, distinguished Córdoba family. ("Don José Antonio"
  is referenced in the "El Comunero" oral-history series; a house stood where the Deportivo Alberdi now is.)
  — ["El Comunero" Relata Pt.1 (Instagram)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DSyDafZii_W/)
- **Streets/limits referenced:** the **río Suquía** to the north, **calle 12 de Octubre** to the south.
  — ["El Comunero" Pt.1](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DSyDafZii_W/)
- **Colonial vestige:** an **18th-century tunnel** (≥15m, function unknown) was discovered in Villa Páez;
  Municipality and Province investigated. *(Great environmental-storytelling hook: a sealed tunnel.)*
  — [La Voz: "Descubren un túnel de la época colonial en Villa Páez"](https://www.lavoz.com.ar/ciudadanos/descubren-un-tunel-de-la-epoca-colonial-en-villa-paez_0_yHUPgegJwd.html)
- **Memory & struggle:** academic work documents residential segregation and the community's resistance.
  — [D'Amico, UCC (PDF)](http://pa.bibdigital.ucc.edu.ar/738/1/A_Damico.pdf)
- **History is oral:** much of the barrio's history is reconstructed from **collective memory of its
  inhabitants** — fiestas, traditions, ways of life. *This fits the game's premise perfectly: the
  vecino tells you what he remembers.*

## 2. Isla de los Patos (starting area)

- **What:** an **artificial island** in the **Río Suquía**, at the height of **Barrio Alberdi**.
- **Inaugurated:** **21 de agosto de 1991**. On opening, hundreds of ducks were released — it became an
  iconic 1990s postcard.
- **Purpose:** recreational space; walks, birdwatching, picnics. ~4.8 acres (~1.9 ha).
- **Current:** under municipal recovery and care.
  — [Turismo Córdoba Capital](https://turismo.cordoba.gob.ar/la-isla-de-los-patos-2/)
  · [Municipalidad de Córdoba (Instagram)](https://www.instagram.com/p/DK0HAcmOHVL/)
  · [Pacer](https://www.mypacer.com/parks/217081/isla-de-los-patos-cordoba)
- *Game hook:* the ducks are why the stray dogs gather; the vecino sits on a bench watching them.

## 3. Cervecería Córdoba (the brewery — the boss location)

- **Founded:** **1912** by **Marín (Martín) Agst** and **Enrique Meyer** (the Meyer family + Agst).
  Building **inaugurated 1° de mayo de 1917**, occupying ~**20.000 m² on the Río Suquía** bank, in
  **Barrio Alberdi**. A symbol of Córdoba's industrialization/modernity.
  — [Museo Cervecería Córdoba](https://museocerveceriacordoba.com.ar/historia-de-la-cerveceria/)
  · [Turismo Córdoba](https://turismo.cordoba.gob.ar/antigua-cerveceria-cordoba/)
  · [UNC Que](https://que.fcc.unc.edu.ar/cerveceria-cordoba-remembranza-de-la-toma-mas-larga-de-una-fabrica-cordobesa/)
- **Location (exact corner):** **Arturo Orgaz y La Tablada**, Barrio Alberdi.
  — [CTA Autónoma Córdoba](https://ctaacordoba.org/las-fabricas-son-de-quienes-las-trabajan/)
- **It also made ice** in its early days.
- **Brands/labels:** **"Córdoba"**, **"Munich"**, **"Bock"** — won the market quickly.
- **Decline:** **1991** concurso de acreedores; **1997** quiebra (bankruptcy).
- **La Toma histórica:** **4 de mayo de 1998** — workers (with families) **took the factory for 105 days**,
  the **longest factory occupation in Argentine labor history**.
  — [La Voz: 27 años de la toma](https://www.lavoz.com.ar/ciudadanos/cordoba-ciudad/los-extrabajadores-de-la-cerveceria-cordoba-conmemoran-los-27-anos-de-la-toma-historica_0_srofReiTYH.html)
  · [vamosporlaliberacion.org](https://vamosporlaliberacion.org/crpmlm/cerveceria-cordoba-105-dias-de-toma-que-marcaron-un-camino/)
- **The chimney:** demolished **15 de abril de 2010** after 83 years — an emblem gone.
  — [Turismo Córdoba](https://turismo.cordoba.gob.ar/antigua-cerveceria-cordoba/)
- **Today:** the site is the **Espacio y Museo Cervecería Córdoba**, preserving the factory, labor
  struggle, and Alberdi identity.
  — [Cadena 3](https://www.cadena3.com/noticia/rony-en-vivo/el-museo-de-la-cerveceria-cordoba-un-viaje-a-un-emblema-de-barrio-alberdi_484098)

*Game hooks:* the boss "el cervecero" is a foreman spirit of the old factory; bottles labeled
Munich/Bock as props; the demolished chimney as a memory a character mourns; the 105-day toma as the
moral weight of the place.

## 4. Cancha de Belgrano / Deportivo Alberdi (the resolution area)

- **Club:** **Club y Biblioteca Popular Recreativo Deportivo Alberdi**. Born **2 de mayo de 2002** from
  the **fusion of two traditional barrio clubs: Argentino Flores and 9 de Julio**. "La unión hace la fuerza."
- **League:** Liga Cordobesa de Fútbol (Primera B). Estadio **C.B.P.R. Deportivo Alberdi** (~1.000 spectators).
- Recently reopened its pool after 30+ years — a barrio meeting point again.
  — [La Voz](https://www.lavoz.com.ar/deportes/futbol/la-union-hace-la-fuerza-la-historia-del-deportivo-alberdi_0_ocGAEH0vam.html)
  · [Futbol Fundaciones](https://futbolfundes.wordpress.com/club-y-biblioteca-popular-recreativo-deportivo-alberdi-cba/)
  · [Fútbol de Córdoba](https://futboldecordoba.com.ar/deportivo-alberdi-recupero-su-pileta-y-vuelve-a-ser-un-punto-de-encuentro-para-el-barrio/)

*Game hook:* the resolution screen at the cancha looks toward Alberdi; the fusion story ("dos clubes,
un barrio") echoes the barrio-togetherness theme.

## 5. The river that ties them together — Río Suquía

Villa Páez borders it, the Isla de los Patos sits in it, the Cervecería stood on its bank. The **Suquía**
is the spine of the whole geography. Use it as the connective tissue between the three maps.

## How to use this in v0.1 (and what to flag as dramatisation)

| Real, documented (safe to state plainly) | Dramatised for the game (flag as fiction) |
|---|---|
| Isla de los Patos built 1991, artificial, ducks released | The vecino's specific dialogue lines |
| Cervecería founded 1912/1917, brands Munich/Bock, made ice, 105-day toma 1998, chimney demolished 2010 | "El cervecero" as a named boss character with a personality |
| Villa Páez since the 1920s, on the Suquía, named for the Páez family | The player being "from" the barrio; the tunnel being openable |
| Deportivo Alberdi = fusion of Argentino Flores + 9 de Julio (2002) | Any match/score details |

**Rule for the dialogue writer:** when an NPC states a historical fact, keep it accurate to column 1.
When the game adds flavor (names, personalities, the tunnel being a gameplay element), that's column 2 —
fictional, and fine, as long as it doesn't contradict column 1.
